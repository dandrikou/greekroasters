# Coffee list and Roasters pages

Scope: site/index.html (coffee list) and site/roasters.html (roasters directory), sharing site/style.css. Visitor mode: Operate. Task: find the cheapest good bean for your taste, then click through to the roaster's shop. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, live data only, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Greek bakaliko (old grocer's shop), where everything is sold by the kilo and the kilo price is the first thing you read. It refuses the category default of a white e-commerce grid with a brown accent.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft-paper tan ground, an espresso-brown header rail with kraft-coloured type, and cards in pale bag-paper with a stitched dashed seam. Ochre-yellow price tags with a punched hole hold black condensed numerals. Olive green is the action and price-drop colour, and terracotta marks rises and stale data. A condensed grotesk is used for numerals and labels, with a system UI face for body text.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole Greek market priced per kilo, narrows it by taste and origin, spots where each bean sits on the price scale, and goes to buy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Espresso header with the brand and the menu. Below it, a kraft band holding the title, a "last weighed" line and a stats strip (coffees, roasters, cheapest per kg). Then a filter bar of standard controls, and the card grid starts within the first viewport on desktop.

FORM: Candidate 3 of 7, the grocer's kilo tag. Signature move: each price tag carries a scale bar marking where this coffee's €/kg sits between the market's cheapest and dearest. seed:522221a424807d9f
